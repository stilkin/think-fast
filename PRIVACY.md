# Privacy Policy — Think Fast!

_Last updated: October 2026_

Think Fast! is a party game by Stilkin. This policy is short because the app
does as little as possible:

- **No data collected.** The app works entirely offline. It collects no
  personal data, runs no analytics, gathers no crash reports, and sends
  nothing to anyone.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** There are no logins, no ads, and no
  third-party services inside the app.
- **Your settings stay on your device.** Language, sound, and game
  preferences are stored in the app's local storage on your device and never
  leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No special permissions.** The app requests no access to your microphone,
  camera, location, contacts, photos, or files.
- **One external link.** The settings screen contains a link to the
  developer's Ko-fi page (ko-fi.com/stilkin). Opening it leaves the app and is
  governed by that site's own policy.
